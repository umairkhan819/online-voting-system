How It Works

Voting on VoteHub takes just four simple steps. From registration to casting your ballot, the entire process is secure, transparent, and built for everyone.

01

Step 1

Register

Create your VoteHub account with your details and verify your identity. Each voter is issued a unique, secure Voter ID.

02

Step 2

Login

Sign in securely using your credentials. Two-factor authentication keeps your account and your vote fully protected.

03

Step 3

Browse Candidates

Explore every candidate's profile, platform, and party. Compare their positions carefully before making your decision.

04

Step 4

Cast Your Vote

Select your candidate and confirm. Your vote is encrypted, recorded instantly, and you receive a verified receipt.
