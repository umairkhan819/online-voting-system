Voting

How do I cast my vote on VoteHub?

After signing in with your Voter ID, go to the Candidates page, review each candidate, and select your choice. Confirm your selection on the review screen and submit. You will receive an on-screen confirmation and a receipt once your vote is recorded.
Voting

Can I change my vote after submitting it?

To ensure integrity, once a vote is submitted and encrypted in the system, it cannot be changed. Please review your choice carefully before final submission.
Security

How does VoteHub keep my vote secure and private?

We use end-to-end encryption and blockchain-inspired verification methods to ensure your identity is decoupled from your specific vote choice.
